## Seenu's answers (Oct 14, 2026)

1. Settings › Payouts keeps the connection status and the payout schedule (the schedule is a Stripe setting). Next and
   recent payouts are hidden until Analytics is built; their component (`PayoutsCard`) stays in `payout-cards.tsx` and
   the API is unchanged. Amended by answer 3: the fees card stays.
2. The Shop section owns `/app/shop`; the parent-shop preview ("View my shop") moves to `/app/shop/preview`.
3. The Fees card stays on Settings › Stripe: it is the only place the 3% rate is stated (D59). So the Stripe page is
   Connection, Payout schedule and Fees; only the next and recent payouts wait for Analytics.

## What changed

- Menu, in order: Home, Jobs, Orders, Price lists, People, Shop (Soon), Analytics (Soon), Settings. Marketing, Money
  and Reports are gone. The `products` key and `/app/products` stay; only its label is now "Price lists".
- People is `/app/people`, opening on Clients. `/app/contacts` and anything under it redirect there (temporary
  redirects in `next.config.ts`, query kept), so old links and menu-search results still work. One rule,
  `/app/contacts/:path*`, covers `/app/contacts` itself too (`:path*` matches zero segments). The redirect can go
  after one release. Contacts, Families and
  Subjects (all jobs) arrive with the People design.
- Settings › Payouts is labelled "Stripe" and shows the connection, the payout schedule and the fees. Its route stays
  `/app/settings/payouts`, because Stripe's return and refresh links point there.
- A job's tabs are Overview, Subjects, Photos, Shop, Orders. The Messages and Settings routes are deleted, so their
  addresses are real 404s; the coming-soon tab component went with them.
- Activity: no menu entry existed; the job's own timeline on Overview stays (D150: timelines on records).
